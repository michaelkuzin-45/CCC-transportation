Transport Categories
Furniture & General Packages
For furniture, antiques, household pieces, estate items, and general packages. CCC reviews description, measurements, weight, photos, number of pieces, glass or marble/stone details, pickup type, delivery ZIP, and requested timeline.
Art & Collectibles
For paintings, framed art, sculptures, statues, fragile collectibles, rare pieces, and high-value items that need careful handling and clear proof-of-condition communication.
Motorcycles
For motorcycle transport including year, make, model, operational status, pickup and delivery details, photos, sender/receiver information, billing details, and timeline.
Vehicles & Trailers
For tow-away or drive-away vehicle and trailer requests. CCC reviews vehicle information, route, who pays, billing info, timeline, and any special transport requirements.
Vintage & High Value
For vintage electronics, coin-operated machines, lamps, porcelains, speakers, antiques, and unusual high-value items requiring extra review before booking.
Custom Shipment Review
For anything that does not fit a standard category. CCC reviews the item, route, handling needs, value, timing, and communication expectations before confirming service.
How Requests Are Reviewed
Quote Information Needed
Item description, dimensions, photos, pickup and delivery locations, ready-to-ship date, desired delivery timeframe, who pays, declared value, and special handling needs.
Handling & Communication
CCC prioritizes careful securement, reviewable written communication, proof-of-condition photos when applicable, and clear expectations before pickup and delivery.
