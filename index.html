Services
Furniture & General Packages
For furniture, antiques, general packages, estate pieces, and delicate items. The quote request asks for description, measurements, weight, photos, number of pieces, glass or marble/stone details, pickup type, delivery ZIP, and desired timeline.
Art & Collectibles
For paintings, framed art, sculptures, statues, collectibles, and high-value pieces. CCC reviews value, dimensions, from/to details, who pays, timeline, and whether additional insurance is needed.
Motorcycles
For motorcycle transport, including year, make, model, operational status, photos, route, sender/receiver information, billing details, and delivery timeline.
Vehicles & Trailers
For tow-away or drive-away vehicle and trailer requests. The form collects year, make, model, from/to information, timeline, who pays, and billing details.
Vintage & High Value Items
For vintage electronics, coin-operated machines, lamps, porcelains, speakers, rare items, and other special-care shipments that need extra review before booking.
Custom Shipment Review
For shipments that do not fit a standard category. CCC reviews the item, route, handling requirements, value, timing, and communication needs before confirming service.
Requesting a Quote Is the Main Action
Every service category routes customers to the quote request form, making it easy to send shipment details, pictures, pickup and delivery information, timeline, value, and billing information.
